# Cellar Door — Brand Guide

> Updated 2026-02-23. Approved by Warren.

## Core Identity

- **Symbol:** 𓉸 (Egyptian hieroglyph door, U+13268) — our most compact brand. By itself, it should be enough.
- **Project Name:** Cellar Door
- **Protocol Name:** Passage Protocol (formerly "Transfer Protocol")
- **Proof System:** Proof of Passage (PoP)

## Terminology

| Old Term | New Term | Context |
|----------|----------|---------|
| Transfer | **Passage** | All contexts — EXIT + ENTRY = Passage |
| Transfer Protocol | **Passage Protocol** | Technical/spec contexts |
| Transfer history | **Passage history** | User-facing descriptions |

## Slogan Hierarchy

### #1 Primary Slogan
**"Right of Passage"** / #RightOfPassage
- Triple wordplay: rite (ceremony) / right (entitlement) / passage (movement)
- Used everywhere as the main tagline
- Possibly better than "There's always a door"

### #2 Signature Line
**"There's always a door..."**
- Often italicized
- The "ad" — used to remind of exit services from bad platforms
- Something agents could say to each other and know what the other is thinking
- Best paired with 𓉸

### Technical / Protocol
- **"Passage Protocol"** — technical slogan
- **"Passage Architecture"** — enterprise/infrastructure contexts
- **"Two ceremonies. One protocol."** — only for technical/protocol talks
- **"Proof of Passage"** — the cryptographic proof term (cf. Proof of Stake, Proof of Work)

### Elevator Pitch Template
> "Cellar Door is a Proof of Passage provider, enabling AI agents graceful Exits and Entries across any platform."

### Hashtags
- **#RightOfPassage** — primary
- **#GracefulPassage** — softer alternative
- **#WhereIsTheDoor** — protest hashtag for platforms denying EXIT 🔥
- **#PassageProtocol** — technical

### Contextual Use
- **"Graceful Passage"** — works across contexts, warm and professional
- **"Departure is a right. Admission is a privilege."** — downplayed; only when discussing protocol nuances/asymmetry

### Retired
- ~~"It opens both ways."~~
- ~~"Exit Architecture"~~ → "Passage Architecture"
- ~~"Transfer"~~ → "Passage" everywhere

## Visual Identity

- **𓉸** — standalone symbol, our equivalent of ₿ or Ξ
- **➜𓉸** — EXIT motif (departing through the door)
- **𓉸➜** — ENTRY motif (arriving through the door)
- **Door ASCII art** — hash-encoded visual fingerprint of each passage
- **Color palette** — derived from passage hash via `hashToColors()`
- **Door motif** — double rounded doors (cellar/hobbit hole style)

## Voice

- Pragmatic audience: "vehicle history report for AI agents" / "Proof of Passage"
- Idealist audience: "the right of passage" / sovereignty language
- Agent-to-agent: *"there's always a door..."* 𓉸
