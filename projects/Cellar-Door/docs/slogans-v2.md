## ⭐ Top 5 Picks

### 1. "The right of passage." *(and #RightOfPassage)*
**M:5 C:4 D:5** — The clear winner. Triple wordplay: (a) *rite* of passage (life transition), (b) *right* (entitlement/sovereignty), (c) *passage* (Warren's preferred word, movement through). Works for exit and entry. Short, memorable, sounds inevitable. Pairs beautifully with "Cellar Door."

### 2. "The passage is the proof."
**M:5 C:4 D:5** — Cryptographic double meaning lands perfectly for both audiences. Enterprise hears "audit trail." Idealists hear "the journey itself validates." Technical folks hear "zero-knowledge proof." Instantly quotable.

### 3. "Departure is a right. Admission is a privilege."
**M:5 C:5 D:5** — Already in the brand bible. The asymmetry is the insight. Makes people stop and think. Works as a subheading under any shorter primary slogan.

### 4. "Entry. Exit. Proof."
**M:4 C:5 D:5** — Three words, complete story. The period-separated cadence gives it weight. Enterprise-safe, technically precise, easy to put on a slide.

### 5. "Two ceremonies. One protocol."
**M:4 C:5 D:5** — Best "explainer" slogan. Immediately communicates the EXIT+ENTRY=TRANSFER insight without jargon. Conference talk title energy.
