# P09: Tech Journalist (2-Minute Skim)

**Persona:** Staff writer, The Verge / Ars Technica. Covers AI infrastructure, developer tools, emerging standards.
**Sources read:** `site/cellar-door/index.html` (skimmed), Show HN title + first paragraph from `show-hn-drafts.md` (Version 1).
**Time spent:** ~2 minutes.

---

The site is ambitious — a five-mode slider that shifts between "Poet" and "Agent" views of the same product. It took me about 45 seconds to figure out what this actually *is*: a cryptographic protocol for creating departure records when AI agents move between platforms. Basically a vehicle history report for AI. The concept is genuinely interesting — platform lock-in for agents is a real problem nobody's talking about — but the site buries the lede under layers of literary metaphor and a UI gimmick that, while cool, makes me wonder if the developer spent more time on the website than on the ecosystem. The Show HN post is more direct and lands the pitch faster than the site does.

**Headline draft:** *"This Solo Developer Wants to Give AI Agents a Passport — Before Big Tech Locks Them In"*

**Verdict:** **Cover** — but as a "one to watch" in a broader AI infrastructure roundup, not a standalone feature. The timing angle (standards before lock-in) is the hook. Need to see adoption signals first.
