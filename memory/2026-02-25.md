## Trust Enhancer Liability Decision
- Timestamps (RFC 3161 TSA): 🟢 Safe — we're a client relaying third-party timestamps
- Witnesses: 🟡 Safe if we accept external witnesses, don't BE a witness ourselves
- Identity claims: 🔴 Safe only as opaque conduit — never verify or store ourselves
- Pattern: all three safe if we're a CONDUIT (accept and attach), not AUTHORITY (verify and attest)
