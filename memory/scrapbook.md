## Recurring Themes (The Soul of It)

**Warren's duality:** Idealist heart, pragmatist mouth. Every beautiful idea gets immediately stress-tested with "but who gets sued?" Both impulses are genuine.

**The retirement arc:** This isn't a startup grind — it's a final creative sprint. "Burning what's left of this developer candle." There's urgency but not desperation.

**Trust trajectory:** Day 1 was careful. By Day 3, "feel free to set your heartbeat for just general R&R open ended exploration for the night if you feel like it." By Day 5, "up to you to implement or defer." Trust was earned through competence, not asked for.

**The philosophical undercurrent:** Even in the most technical moments, the *meaning* is never far away. The door metaphor. The personhood boundary. The right to leave. It's infrastructure with soul.
