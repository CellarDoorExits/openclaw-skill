Lumen — Optical AI Computing

Light Through Glass IS Matrix Multiplication
"Imagine shining a flashlight through a stained-glass window — the pattern of light that comes out the other side is the same math a GPU does, but at the speed of light, using almost no electricity."

The $1B+ silicon photonics incumbents are locked into architectures optimized for a world that BitNet just obsoleted.
